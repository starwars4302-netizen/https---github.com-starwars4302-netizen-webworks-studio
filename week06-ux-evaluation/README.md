## 2. Five UX Findings

Document **exactly five meaningful findings**.

### Finding 1
**Observation:**  
total omission of time durations and exact mileage

**Evidence:**  
featured trail cards and text breakdowns utilize descriptions but lack metrics for time length

**User Impact:**  
this would impact knowledge on the trail itself and prevent her from going to the trail due to not understanding rules and possible fees that may apply 

**Principle:**  
effeciency and task completion

**Priority:** high

**Recommendation:**  
add a summary to each trail containing mtrics and distance

### Finding 2
**Observation:** 
critical information such as rules, hours, and prices  
**Evidence:**  
text states "dogs may be allowed depending on park rules" and "fees and park hours are subject to change"
**User Impact:**  
user can be left anxious, hesitant and would be left to look further for information regarding the park information to clarify the rules and fees
**Principle:**  
information clarity
**Priority:**  
medium
**Recommendation:**  
replace placeholder warnings with explicit and definative text

### Finding 3
**Observation:**  
information shown is compressed into large paragraphs than properly structured into analytical elements
**Evidence:**  
the trail information such as "juniper creek loop" consist entirely of multiple paragraphs
**User Impact:**  
it impacts the users scanning abilities and prevents the understanding of the information provided word for word. this can primarily be a problem on mobile phone use.
**Principle:**  
analytical and flexibility
**Priority:**  
medium
**Recommendation:**  
restructure trail breakdown and using bulleted points or a grid format

### Finding 4
**Observation:**  
intimidating langauge to desribe trail is unexplained and noncontextual
**Evidence:**  
the description for "Painted Bluff Trail" uses phrasing such as "the upper bluff is exposed" and words such as "strenuous"
**User Impact:**  
for someone as inexperienced as Maya this can be troubling due to the lack of understanding of the terms, phrasings and references within the text to describe the trails
**Principle:**  
plain language
**Priority:**  
medium
**Recommendation:**  
uitilize more user and beginner friendly language or provide clear expanations of the phrases to provide familiarity on the terms

### Finding 5
**Observation:**  
lack of imagery and visuals to provide visual insite of the trails
**Evidence:**  
the whole webpage lacks and visual imagery of the trails described and nothing to back up the description provided in the text
**User Impact:**  
this can be misleading due to the lck of visual reference of the trails and prevdnt the posibility of looking further into the trails and would lead to further external searching
**Principle:**  
visual context
**Priority:**  
high
**Recommendation:**  
replace the solid color boxes with images of the trails being described to provide visual reference
